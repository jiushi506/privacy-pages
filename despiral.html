Privacy Policy

DeSpiral

Last updated: June 11, 2026

DeSpiral is designed to work without an account or backend service. The app keeps your input and preferences on your device and does not send your text to a server.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information, message content, contact lists, location data, or analytics data from your use of the app.

Information Stored On Device

The app may store the following locally on your device:

  • Your app preferences, such as sound, haptics, reminder status, and reminder time.
  • Reminder permission state, so the app can keep your daily reminder working.
  • Basic usage state such as whether the intro has been shown.

How Your Text Is Handled

Anything you type into DeSpiral is processed locally to power the app's effect. It is not uploaded, stored on our servers, or shared with third parties.

Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. Notification timing and delivery are handled locally by the operating system.

Third Parties

DeSpiral does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or account services. The app may rely on platform-provided components from Apple or Google to support notifications and sharing.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data because we do not collect it.

Children's Privacy

DeSpiral is not intended to collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us so we can review the issue.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy if the app changes. Any new version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@despiral.app.
